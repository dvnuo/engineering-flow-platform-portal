These settings apply to the devices you record on; the defaults suit a public BrowserStack account. Recording devices run Appium 2.19.0 unless you set another version; BrowserStack's own default, Appium 1.22.0, does not know the Inspector's Back, Home, and App switch buttons. Choose a private network only when the app talks to servers BrowserStack's devices cannot reach. BrowserStack ends a session after the idle timeout without a command, 300 seconds at most; the local bridge keeps the device alive while you record, so leave this at 300 unless your team says otherwise.
