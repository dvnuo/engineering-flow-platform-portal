## How to start

1. Choose an assistant type — the kind of work you do. Your administrator defines these.
2. Give it a name. Everything else is filled in for you.
3. Open Connectors and supply your own credentials for the services you need.

## What an assistant is

A running workspace you chat with. It keeps its own conversation history and
files, and it acts using your credentials.

Assistants are personal. Creating your own from a shared type is the normal way
to work; you do not share one with a colleague.

## Simple and advanced

Simple mode asks for a name and a type. That is enough for almost everyone.

Advanced setup exposes the engine and the behavior and skill branches. Use it
only if you have been told to.
